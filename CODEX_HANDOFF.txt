AGREED MOVEMENT AND CAMERA BEHAVIOR
- X is horizontal travel, Z is height, Y is constrained to zero.
- Uses CharacterMovementComponent calculations and swept collision, not a
  physics-simulated rigid body. Ground movement has fast acceleration/braking.
- Normal airborne control is direct: release input to stop horizontal motion,
  reverse immediately, and preserve analog stick strength.
- Jump press starts a jump; holding extends ascent up to JumpMaxHoldTime.
- A short buffer remembers a jump pressed just before landing. A released
  buffered tap still gives a short jump. Hold state controls additional height.
- Jump buffer uses GetTimeSeconds consistently for creation and expiry.
- Descending gravity is stronger than ascending gravity.
- Flat-base floor checks are enabled. User reported wall/box-edge sticking,
  especially around ascent and landing; enabling this improved it and the user
  accepted the result. Collision remains a capsule, not a rectangular collider.
- No double jump. The user wants to experiment with level design first.
- Air dash: once per landing, airborne only, fixed horizontal direction from
  current input or the last facing direction when input is neutral.
- During dash: rapid X movement, zero vertical velocity and no gravity-driven
  Z movement. Swept collision remains active; a blocking hit ends the dash.
- After dash: gravity resumes from zero vertical velocity and momentum-based
  air control lasts UNTIL LANDING. A rejected dash must not change air control.
- Landing recharges dash and restores direct air control. A successful dash
  clears an earlier buffered jump request and ends held-jump assistance.
- Camera has absolute rotation, follows the character directly, does not rotate
  with facing, and has a downward pitch. Camera lag/collision testing are off.
- Entering the arena blends to its fixed camera; leaving blends back to the
  player. User wants a quick smooth transition and approved the tested result.

PLATFORM COLOR IMPLEMENTATION
- Source/RGB/Platforms/RGBColorComponent.h/.cpp:
  URGBColorComponent owns ERGBColor (Red, Green, Blue), CurrentColor,
  InitialColor, StartingColor and bRandomizeInitialColor (default false).
  SetColor validates enum, ignores unchanged values and broadcasts
  FRGBColorChangedSignature OnColorChanged(PreviousColor, NewColor).
  AdvanceColor cycles Red -> Green -> Blue -> Red.
  BeginPlay calls ResetColor. ResetColor uses InitialColor unless randomness is
  enabled, then selects one of the three with RandRange(0,2) EACH time.
